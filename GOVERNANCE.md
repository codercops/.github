# Governance

This covers the public CODERCOPS projects: [chatcops](https://github.com/codercops/chatcops), [ogcops](https://github.com/codercops/ogcops), [toolbelt](https://github.com/codercops/toolbelt) and [discord-bot](https://github.com/codercops/discord-bot).

## Who decides

[@anurag629](https://github.com/anurag629) is the lead maintainer of all four: the one who reviews and merges pull requests, cuts releases and has the final say when people disagree. Disagreements are talked through on the issue or PR, in public, before that.

## Maintainers

These contributors have Triage access. They can label, assign, close and reopen issues and pull requests, and ask for reviews. They can't merge or push.

Maintainers still claim issues and open PRs like any other contributor, and their merged PRs count on the [monthly leaderboard](https://oss.codercops.com/leaderboard).

### Becoming a maintainer

There's no form. We invite people who have had at least 10 PRs merged across our repos, have been active in the last 60 days, and review or help others on issues and on Discord. If you think you're there, say so in `#oss-help` on [Discord](https://discord.gg/mpGdJyjaRQ).

After 90 days without activity, Triage access is removed. It comes back when you do.

## Money

The projects are MIT licensed and free. CODERCOPS is a software studio, but it doesn't sell these projects or a hosted version of them, and it earns nothing from them. Today CODERCOPS pays for their hosting and the lead maintainer's time.

Sponsorship and grants go to two things: paid time for reviewing, triage and releases, and hosting (Cloudflare, domains, and the AI API usage behind the public demos). Our funding needs are published as a [funding.json manifest](https://oss.codercops.com/funding.json). You can sponsor through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/anurag629), or write to hello@codercops.com.
